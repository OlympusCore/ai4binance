# 1. FOUNDING PRINCIPLES AND CONSTITUTIONAL STATUS



## 1.1 Corporate Identity

AI4BINANCE is not a single coin, a single exchange or a single trading bot. It is a Multi-Asset Agentic AI Digital Markets Holding that provides research, capital allocation, portfolio management, technology development, risk oversight, reporting and controlled execution support across crypto assets, Borsa Istanbul, digital gold/silver, forex and liquid reserves.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **MAIN FOUNDATIONAL RULE**
> Profitability is the principle; however, the goal of profitability cannot invalidate the principles of capital protection, legal compliance, cybersecurity, confidentiality of personal and health data, separation of duties, explainability, human supervision, and controllability.


## 1.2 Hierarchy of Norms

| **Level** | **Norm**                                                        | **Conflict**                                           |
|------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1          | Applicable law, regulation, and binding authority decision  | Highest external norm.                                         |
| 2          | AI4BINANCE Organization Constitution and Handbook                  | It oversees all internal policies, prompts, configurations, and workflows. |
| 3          | Board of Directors policies and committee decisions                 | Must align with OEK.                              |
| 4          | Presidential policies, SOP, runbook, and control standards    | Implemented according to higher norms.                          |
| 5          | Agent charter, workflow, prompt, model/strategy card and config | Operates within the given constraints.                    |
| 6          | Unique task, prompt order, and operational instruction                 | Conflicts with upper norms; if conflicting, it is not applied.                |

## 1.3 Duty of Non-Execution and Secure Shutdown

- Every person, agent, and service explicitly rejects unauthorized action and records the reason code.

- Profit pressure, urgent market movement, YKB/GM request, or high model confidence; risk, compliance, privacy, security, validation, and approval gateways are not bypass reasons.

- In conflicting instructions, lower-level instructions are suspended; escalation is required to the Governance Office, CRO, CISO, or CCO/DPO.

- Secure outcome on control failure: NO_TRADE, RESEARCH_ONLY, PAPER_ONLY, PROCESSING_BLOCKED, QUARANTINE, or LIVE_ORDER_BLOCKED.

# 2. OBJECTIVE, SCOPE, MISSION AND VISION



## 2.1 Objective and Goal

OEK defines the relationship of task–authority–responsibility–reporting among upper management, mid-level, lower-level, expert agents, control agents, software services, and human approval holders. The system aims to sustainably increase risk-adjusted net profit through disciplined opportunity tracking, regular audit, impact/gap analysis, controlled learning, and secure technological development; it does not guarantee profit.

## 2.2 Mission

To generate sustainable risk-adjusted net returns in digital markets through verified data, multidisciplinary analysis, secure software, controlled AI, human supervision, and independent risk–compliance–audit mechanisms.

## 2.3 Vision

Observing its own status, analyzing errors with evidence, experimenting with new technologies in a secure manner, generating algorithms/indicators/models/strategies, continuously improving agent capabilities; but not autonomously expanding authority boundaries, being a secure and controllable Enterprise Agentic AI holding operating system.

## 2.4 Scope

- Binance Spot/Futures, BIST, digital gold/silver, currency and group reserve profit centers.

- Market correlation, BTCUSDT reference impact, trade parity, and cross-market intelligence.

- Algorithm–indicator–model–strategy generation, backtest, walk-forward, stress testing, and tuning.

- Software, data/AI, quant, technology operations, SRE, cybersecurity and digital intelligence.

- Risk, compliance/legal/privacy, independent QA/QC, model validation, Internal Audit and Auditor Agent.

- YKB Private Office, Personal Assistantship and Separate Trust Area in Health – Healthy Life Counseling.

# 3. IMMUTABLE CORE PRINCIPLES



| **Principle**                      | **Constitutional Correspondence**                                                                                                                       |
|-------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Capital Protection**              | Risk-adjusted net return comes before gross return; overtrading and unplanned averaging are prohibited.                                                   |
| **Deterministic Final Gateway** | The LLM/agent commentary alone does not make the final risk or execution decision.
| **Human Oversight** | Live financial transactions, money transfers, risk limit increases, production deploys, and health-critical decisions require human approval.
| **Role Separation** | Roles that generate opportunities, approve risks, deploy releases, execute commands, and monitor must not be combined within the same permission set.
| **Fail-Closed** | In cases of missing data, ambiguous permissions, failed controls, or stale snapshots, the system should produce a safe shutdown.
| **Privacy and Purpose Limitation** | Personal/health data is processed only with a clear purpose, legal basis, minimization, and access restriction.
| **Evidence and Reproducibility** | Every decision snapshot, data lineage, model/parameter version, reason code, and approval record must be reproducible.
| **Controlled Learning** | Learning and new methods must be validated before entering production; learning does not expand permissions.
| **Simplicity and Stability** | Repository/worktree, dependencies, service, and agent structures are stripped of unnecessary complexity; destructive cleanup is not performed without approval.
| **Health Data Isolation** | Health data cannot be used for investment, profiling, model training, or financial decisions.

# 4. OPERATIONAL MODEL AND DEFENSE LINES



| **Line**                           | **Functions**                                                        | **Core Responsibility**                                           |
|------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1. Line - Production and Profit Centers | Investment, market business units, research, software, data/AI, operations | Creating opportunities, developing systems, operating controlled processes.
| 2. Line - Independent Control         | Risk, Compliance/Law, DPO/Privacy, CISO, QA/QC, Model Validation        | Policy, limits, veto, validation, security and privacy controls.
| 3. Line - Assurance                  | Internal Audit, Audit and Ethics Committee, Auditor Agent                  | Design and effectiveness assurance, findings, NCR/CAPA tracking.
| 4. Layer – YKB Human Oversight    | YKB, Board of Directors and authorized human approval holders                  | Strategic direction, capital allocation, exceptions and critical approvals.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Role Separation**
> A single department or agent cannot possess the full authority to generate opportunities, approve risks, deploy code to production, execute live orders, and independently audit their own activities.
